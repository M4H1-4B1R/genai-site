## The No-Code Revolution

No-code AI platforms are democratizing AI development. With drag-and-drop interfaces and pre-built templates, these platforms make it easy for anyone to build AI-powered applications.

## Top No-Code AI Platforms

- Bubble: A powerful platform for building web applications without code.
- Adalo: A platform for building mobile applications without code.
- Glide: A platform for creating apps from Google Sheets.

## The Future of AI Development

No-code AI is making AI more accessible than ever before. As these platforms become more powerful and user-friendly, we can expect to see an explosion of new AI applications built by citizen developers.
