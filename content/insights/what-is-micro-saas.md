What is a Micro SaaS?

A micro-SaaS is a small-scale Software as a Service (SaaS) business that is designed to address a specific problem for a niche market. These businesses are typically run by an individual or a small team, often without any external funding.

Key Characteristics of a Micro-SaaS:

- Niche Focus: Micro-SaaS businesses target a specific audience with a specialized solution, which can lead to a dedicated customer base. This focus helps them avoid direct competition with larger, more established SaaS companies.
- Small Scale: They are typically managed by one person or a small team and have a smaller user base compared to traditional SaaS companies.
- Minimal Resources: The business model emphasizes efficiency and low overhead costs, often operating without the need for significant initial investment. Many founders work remotely, further reducing costs.
- Subscription-Based: Like traditional SaaS, micro-SaaS businesses usually operate on a recurring revenue model, charging customers a subscription fee.
- Agile and Adaptable: Because of their small size and focused scope, micro-SaaS businesses can quickly adapt to market changes and customer feedback.

Why Build a Micro SaaS?

Building a Micro SaaS has many advantages. It offers a recurring revenue stream, the flexibility to work from anywhere, and the satisfaction of solving a real problem for a specific audience. It's a great way to enter the SaaS market without the need for significant funding or a large team.
