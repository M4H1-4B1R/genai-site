## The Software Company Dilemma

If you're a custom software company in 2026, you face a weird problem: clients are asking "why can't AI just build this?" And for some projects, they're right to ask.

The answer isn't to pretend AI doesn't exist. It's to offer both: AI-built solutions for simple projects, and custom engineering for everything else. That's exactly what we did at GenAI Labs with Jetpack.

## The Two-Track Model

Here's how we think about it:

- Track 1: Jetpack (AI Builder) — For MVPs, prototypes, internal tools, and micro SaaS. Client gets a working product in hours, not months. Cost: fraction of custom development.
- Track 2: Custom Engineering — For complex platforms, enterprise integrations, compliance-heavy applications, and products that need to scale to millions. Client gets a properly architected system built by senior engineers.

These aren't competing offerings — they're a pipeline. Many of our best custom development clients started with a Jetpack prototype to validate their idea before investing in the full build.

## Why This Makes Business Sense

For the software company:

- You capture clients at every budget level — not just the ones who can afford $50K+ builds
- Jetpack prototypes become upsell opportunities for custom development
- Your engineers focus on the challenging, high-value work they actually enjoy
- Faster turnaround on simple projects means happier clients and more referrals

For the client:

- They can test their idea for nearly nothing before committing to a big build
- Simple projects get delivered in days, not months
- They have a clear upgrade path when they outgrow the AI-built version
- One company handles everything — no vendor juggling

## What We've Learned

After running both tracks, here's what surprised us:

- 40% of Jetpack users eventually need custom work. The AI builder isn't replacing our engineering team — it's feeding it.
- Clients trust you more when you're honest. Telling someone "you don't need a $30K custom build — try Jetpack first" builds more trust than overselling.
- Engineers are happier. Nobody wants to build yet another CRUD dashboard. Let AI handle the boring stuff.

## The Honest Truth About AI Builders

AI builders are incredible for what they do. But they don't replace software engineers. They replace the need for software engineers on projects that were never complex enough to justify them in the first place.

The admin dashboard that was going to cost $15K and take 3 months? AI builds it in an afternoon. The fintech platform with regulatory compliance, real-time fraud detection, and bank integrations? You need engineers for that.

Smart software companies embrace both. The ones that pretend AI doesn't exist will lose the simple projects. The ones that pretend AI can do everything will lose credibility.
