## 10 Untapped B2C SaaS Niches for 2025

As of 2025, the B2C SaaS market is ripe with opportunities for entrepreneurs who can identify and cater to specific, often underserved, consumer needs. The overarching trends point towards hyper-personalization, the integration of artificial intelligence (AI), and a focus on niche communities and sustainable living. Here are some of the most promising untapped B2C SaaS niches:

- Hyper-Personalized Financial Planning: AI-powered financial advisors and niche financial management tools for specific demographics.
- AI-Driven Mental Wellness: AI-powered mental health companions and personalized meditation and mindfulness apps.
- Hyper-Personalized Education: AI-powered learning experience platforms (LXPs) and niche skill development platforms.
- Sustainable Living and Ethical Consumption: Carbon footprint tracking apps and ethical shopping assistants.
- Niche-Specific Social and Community Platforms: Hobby-specific networks and support-oriented communities.
- Personalized Nutrition and Fitness: AI-powered meal planning and personalized workout apps.
- Smart Home Automation for Wellness: SaaS for managing smart home devices to improve sleep, air quality, and overall well-being.
- Subscription Box Management: Tools for consumers to manage their various subscriptions in one place.
- Personalized Travel Planning: AI-powered travel recommendation engines and itinerary builders.
- Digital Legacy Management: Platforms for managing digital assets and online accounts after death.
