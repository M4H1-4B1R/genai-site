What is a Micro SaaS?

A Micro SaaS is a small, often niche-focused Software as a Service business. Unlike large SaaS companies, Micro SaaS businesses are typically run by one or a few people and target a specific audience with a specific solution.

Why Build a Micro SaaS?

Building a Micro SaaS has many advantages. It offers a recurring revenue stream, the flexibility to work from anywhere, and the satisfaction of solving a real problem for a specific audience. It's a great way to enter the SaaS market without the need for significant funding or a large team.

## Finding Your Niche

The key to a successful Micro SaaS is finding the right niche. Look for a problem that a specific group of people is willing to pay to solve.
